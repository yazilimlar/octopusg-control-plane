## Design

`src/graphview.ts` holds the whole map as pure functions:

- **Lanes** — AgoraXAI · Platforms · Artemis · Products · Ventures · Labs and archive ·
  Repositories · Checkouts. A product's lane comes from its taxonomy, so the Artemis and
  AgoraXAI Atlas peer distinction survives: they are different lanes, never nested.
- **Layout** — `layout(nodes)` returns placed nodes, lane rules and the canvas size. Positions
  are a function of lane order, count and canvas width; no randomness, no clock, no storage.
- **Encodings** — `colourClass` / `colourValue` for one dimension at a time; `edgeDash` and
  `edgeStyleLabel` for truth; `edgeLegend` renders the patterns as text.
- **Presets and the relationship filter** — the v0.1 vocabulary (dependency, ownership,
  historical) maps onto typed edges, plus `resource` in the repository preset.

The map view keeps every existing control and DOM hook (`#map-focus`, `#map-kind`, zoom, reset,
`[data-node]`, `.edge`), so the v0.1 interactions are unchanged; "Reset map" now also returns the
preset and colour dimension to their defaults.
