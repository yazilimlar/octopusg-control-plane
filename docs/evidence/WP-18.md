## Scope and authority

This design-only package is authorized by [S11](../sources/S11-2026-09-22-owner-wp18-authorization.md).
It defines the Supabase-specific boundary without accessing Supabase, credentials, providers or
the DayOS backend. WP-17 remains reserved for later implementation after G3.

## Design

- `src/supabase-boundary.ts` validates canonical project-identity resolution shape, the
  metadata-only allowlist (including authorization state), Level 1 ceiling, stale/ambiguous
  identity blocking and not-authorized G3 draft.
- `ADR-0009` defines identity resolution, scopes, prohibited surfaces, failure/redaction,
  revocation and G3 requirements.
- `ADR-0005` records the immutable-identifier approval rule.

## Requirements

`OG-CONN-015` remains PROPOSED. WP-18 defines the criteria for its later advancement to
APPROVED IMPLEMENTATION TARGET; no connector, provider, G3 authorization or observation is created.
