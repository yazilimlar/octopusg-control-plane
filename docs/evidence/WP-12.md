**Derivation, in full.** The lockup is the master cropped to the artwork's own bounds
(84,154)–(1170,1186) and resized to 600 × 570. The symbol is the master cropped to the symbol band
(84,154)–(1170,860) and centred on a square transparent canvas — padding, never stretching, so the
aspect ratio is exact — then resized to 256. The favicon is that same square canvas at 64. Every
resize is Lanczos. Nothing was recoloured, re-laid-out, redrawn or traced.

**Metadata.** The master keeps the `eXIf` and `sRGB` chunks the owner's file carried. Every served
derivative was rebuilt pixel-by-pixel onto a fresh canvas, so it carries no `eXIf`, `tEXt`, `iTXt`,
`zTXt`, `iCCP` or `tIME` chunk at all — asserted by reading the PNG chunk list in the test, not by
trusting the encoder.

**Original attachment filename.** Not recoverable. The attachment reached this session under the
generated name `3b5576cc-image.png` and the owner did not state an original filename. The record
says so rather than inventing one.

**Provenance, exactly as the owner stated it.** Owner-supplied and owner-authorized for use in
OctopusG; produced through owner-directed ChatGPT image-generation iterations; classified as a
proprietary project brand asset supplied and authorized by the owner. Third-party authorship,
third-party licensing, copyright registration and legal exclusivity are explicitly **not** claimed,
and `loadBrandManifest` refuses a record that ever adds such a claim (a test adds `licence: MIT`
and asserts the refusal).

## Where the brand appears

| Context | What is shown | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sidebar | compact symbol (46 px) above the real text **OctopusG** and the existing sub-line | A compact context: no wordmark image, no tagline, and the product name stays selectable text with an empty `alt` on the decorative symbol. |
| Portfolio heading | the full lockup at 272 px on a light plate | The only place with room for the wordmark **and** the tagline to be legible. `alt="OctopusG — Architect-Engineer of Complex Systems"`. |
| Footer | the tagline as **real text** | So the approved wording is readable, selectable and available to a screen reader, not only inside an image. |
| Browser tab | `favicon.png`, with `favicon.svg` kept as a second `rel="icon"` | A faithful 64 px reduction of the existing symbol; nothing was redrawn for it. |
| Below 900 px | the lockup is hidden; the symbol carries the brand | The tagline would not be legible, and the owner said it need not appear in compact contexts. |

**Contrast.** The wordmark is dark navy and would fail against the application's dark background,
so the lockup sits on a light plate (`#f4f8fa`). The asset itself is never recoloured or inverted —
that is the decision recorded in ADR-0006 §6, so it is not "fixed" later by altering the mark.

## What did not change

- **No semantic colour.** Map encodings, truth kinds, risk bands, connector state and status badges
  are untouched; `src/brand.ts` contains no colour value at all, asserted by a test, so a brand
  colour cannot leak into an encoding.
- **No stored state.** The localStorage key `agoraxai.octopus.workflow.v1`, the schema constant
  `Octopus` in `data/schema.json`, the snapshot classification and the export shape are unchanged,
  each asserted by a test.
- **No other screen.** The lockup appears on the Portfolio view only; a browser check asserts it is
  absent elsewhere. WP-09 and WP-11 functionality was not touched.
- **Nothing is fetched.** Every image path is a local absolute path; `scripts/serve.mjs` names the
  three brand files one by one — it has no directory handler and no path joining, so a traversal
  request 404s. The browser check asserts every `<img>` on the page is local and actually decoded.

## Open questions

- **OG-SEC-008 is still pending, deliberately.** It must not be marked verified until the owner has
  placed the final complete-history bundle in an off-device location, and v0.2 is not frozen until
  that copy is confirmed.
- The brand is a first application. A future mark means a new master, regenerated derivatives and
  an updated manifest, not an edit in place.
- The light plate behind the lockup is a deliberate exception in a dark interface. If the owner
  would rather have a dark-surface variant of the artwork, that is a new owner-supplied asset, not
  a recolouring OctopusG may perform.
